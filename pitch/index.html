Backup · honest limits and hard questions

What we say before a judge asks.

Why Hedera?

The network runs our contract at the release time by itself, the public log is ordered and costs a fraction of a cent, and fees are fixed. Elsewhere we would need a separate keeper service.

Isn’t this a guardian wallet?

Same family of idea, and we do not claim to have invented it. Ours targets a deceived owner, adds physical devices, and lets the network execute the release.

Could your server steal or block?

It holds no key that can pay. Every action needs a device signature that the vault checks itself. The release is booked on Hedera’s own timer.

What does it not protect against?

A patient scam that fools both the owner and the guardian. An ESP32 is not a bank-grade secure chip. This is testnet software, not yet audited.

What is simulated?

The scam call and the money: a test token stands in for USDC. The contract, the scheduled release and the public log are real, on Hedera testnet.

Did you talk to users?

Not yet. It is the first thing on our list after the hackathon.
